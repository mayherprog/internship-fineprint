## Quoted third-party material

Every `quote` field in `data/` is text written and published by the firm named
in that record. It is reproduced verbatim, in short extracts, for
identification, verification and reference — so that a reader can confirm what
a firm actually said rather than take this project's word for it.

That material remains the property of its authors. It is **not** relicensed by
this project, and neither the MIT licence nor CC BY 4.0 grants you any right to
it. Each quote carries the source URL it came from and the date it was read.

If you are a rights holder and want an extract removed or corrected, open an
issue and it will be handled promptly.

## What this project does not claim

- **Not advice.** Nothing here is legal, immigration, employment or career
  advice. Work authorisation in particular is fact-specific and should be
  checked with a qualified adviser and with your institution.
- **Not current.** Every row is a snapshot with a `checked` date. Firms rewrite
  careers pages without notice. **The firm's own current page is always the
  authority.**
- **Not complete.** Coverage is partial and openly reported as such. Rows marked
  `unverified` mean nobody has checked, not that a firm has no policy.
- **Not a verdict.** Rows record what a firm states about eligibility. They do
  not determine whether any particular person is eligible, and must not be read
  as doing so.
- **Silence is not a finding about a firm.** `silent` means a specific page was
  read and did not address the question. It is not evidence that no internal
  policy exists; applicant tracking systems can enforce rules that are never
  published.

## Sourcing exclusions

Test-prep vendors and content farms are excluded from citation at every tier.
They may be read as leads; they are never recorded as evidence. Where a widely
circulated figure could not be traced to a firm's own words, it is deliberately
absent from this dataset rather than recorded at a lower tier.
