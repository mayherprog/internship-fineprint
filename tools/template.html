Internship eligibility, in the firms' own words

Every row is a sentence a firm published about who may apply, quoted exactly, with a link and the date it was read. Firms that say nothing are recorded as saying nothing.

Read this first. This is a dated snapshot, not advice, and not a verdict about you. The firm's own current page is always the authority — pages get rewritten without notice. “Publishes nothing” is a fact about the public record only: it does not mean a firm has no such policy, and silence is neither permission nor prohibition.

Your answers stay on this page — nothing is stored, sent, or written to the address bar. Matching compares your answers with criteria firms have stated, in a dated snapshot. It is not an eligibility determination.

These groups compare your answers with criteria firms have stated, as recorded on the dates shown. “Exclude” means a stated criterion, as written on the date it was read, does not cover your answers — it is not a verdict about you, and the firm's current page is the authority. The largest group is usually the one nobody has verified: treat it as unread, not unavailable.
